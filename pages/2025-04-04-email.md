This website does not contain my email address.

This has to do with my personal policy, which is to make it slightly difficult to reach me; slightly, because, in reality, it is trivial task to find a way to contact me. I just decided not to post it upfront.

I forgot where I initially got this idea, but this policy served me quite well over the years. Now, most of the emails I receive are newsletters that I actively subscribe to, or from real people reaching out to me through my own work.

:::{aside}
A few people ended up calling me, and to this day I am not really sure how they found my phone number. Life finds a way, I suppose.
:::
